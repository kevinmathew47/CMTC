01 — About the parish
Read our history →

Rooted in faith since Easter 1997.

Christos Mar Thoma Church is a parish of the Malankara Mar Thoma Syrian Church, serving families across Kakkanad, Thrikkakara and the surrounding areas. What began in 1997 as a small congregation of 71 families has grown into a vibrant, fast-growing parish in the heart of the cyber city.

Whether you are a member, a newcomer to Kochi or a visitor, you are warmly welcome to join us in worship, fellowship and service.
